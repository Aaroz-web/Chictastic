03 — ABOUT NOCLUE ABROAD

Travel without knowing the destination.

NoClue Abroad was created from the idea that travel should be easy, but still feel exciting. The customer tells us what kind of experience they want, and we take care of finding a suitable trip without requiring them to choose the destination themselves.

Our idea

The service is designed for people who want to travel but do not want to spend hours planning. It also suits adventurous travellers who enjoy the uncertainty of a surprise.

Our approach

We consider wishes, budget, dates and interests when building the trip. The goal is a good price-quality ratio and a simple customer experience.

Our audience

Busy people who dislike planning, as well as young and adventurous travellers looking for something different.

Our position

NoClue Abroad is built from a Nordic perspective, with departures from Finland and Sweden and a focus on safe and environmentally conscious destinations.

04 — BUSINESS PLAN

A simple idea with a clear customer promise.

Problem

Trip planning can take a lot of time. Many people want to travel but do not want to compare destinations, flights, hotels and activities themselves.

Solution

NoClue Abroad turns the customer's wishes into a surprise trip. The customer decides the important limits while the destination remains a mystery.

Revenue model

Revenue can come from the margin included in the trip price and from carefully selected travel services and activities.

Marketing

The brand focuses on social media, a clear visual identity and the unusual surprise-travel concept. The initial marketing budget is €2,000.

05 — FINANCIAL PLAN

Keeping the numbers simple.

Initial marketing

€2,000 is allocated to launch marketing and customer acquisition.

Main costs

Costs include marketing, website and technical services, payment and booking-related costs, and the operational work needed to build each trip.

Pricing principle

The price should cover the trip costs and operating work while leaving room for a sustainable margin.

Growth

Growth depends on gaining awareness, building a customer base and keeping the service attractive as the number of bookings increases.

06 — SWOT ANALYSIS

The business in four views.

STRENGTHS

Distinct concept

A surprise-travel service built from a Nordic perspective, with a youthful point of view and attention to safe and environmentally conscious destinations.

WEAKNESSES

New company

A young business has less experience and needs time to build awareness and a customer base.

OPPORTUNITIES

Nordic market

The concept can stand out in the Nordic market and can later expand to more customers and markets.

THREATS

Market size

If the service remains focused only on the Nordic market, the smaller customer base can limit growth.

07 — PEER FEEDBACK

What people thought about the idea.

The idea was described as interesting and distinctive. The surprise-trip concept can make travelling more exciting while saving planning time. The service takes wishes, budget and interests into account, and it can be especially suitable for young and adventurous travellers. The feedback also highlighted the potential of the concept.

08 — RESPONSIBILITY

Travel with responsibility.

Environment

Environmental responsibility is considered when choosing destinations and activities. The aim is to favour sensible travel choices and avoid unnecessary impact where possible.

Safety

Safety is an important part of destination selection. The service aims to choose destinations and activities that are suitable for the customer's wishes and travel situation.

Customer responsibility

Customers receive the information they need before the trip, including practical instructions and a packing list.

Transparency

The destination is a surprise, but the important conditions of the trip are agreed with the customer beforehand.
