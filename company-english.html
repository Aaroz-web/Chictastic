OUR COMPANY

Travel should be exciting, not complicated.

NoClue Abroad is a Nordic travel company creating personalized surprise trips for people who want to travel without planning everything themselves. We combine the freedom of spontaneous travel with the convenience of having the important details taken care of.

01 — THE IDEA

A new way to travel.

Customers tell us their wishes, budget, travel dates and interests, and we use them to create a suitable trip. Instead of choosing the destination first, the traveller chooses the kind of experience they want. This makes the trip feel more personal while keeping the most exciting part — discovering where you are going.

02 — HOW IT WORKS

You know what you want.
We find where.

We consider the traveller’s dates, budget, interests and practical wishes when building the trip. The goal is not simply to find a random destination, but to create a trip that makes sense for that particular traveller. The destination remains a surprise until the agreed reveal moment, making the journey itself part of the experience.

03 — OUR CUSTOMERS

Made for curious travellers.

Our service is designed for busy people who do not want to spend hours planning, as well as adventurous travellers who enjoy discovering something unexpected. It is for people who want the excitement of not knowing everything in advance, without having to worry about organizing every detail themselves.

04 — OUR POSITION

A Nordic view on mystery travel.

We focus on customers from Finland and the Nordic countries and want to build a fresh, youthful and recognizable Nordic travel brand. Our perspective comes from the Nordic way of valuing simplicity, safety, nature and good experiences. We want NoClue Abroad to feel modern and approachable rather than like a traditional travel agency.

05 — RESPONSIBILITY

Travel with care.

Safety and responsible choices are important parts of how we plan our trips, including the destinations and experiences we recommend. Where possible, we favour local businesses, public transport and experiences that respect local culture, nature and animals. We also pay attention to destinations where travellers can feel safe and comfortable.

06 — THE FUTURE

More experience.
Less planning.

Our goal is to make personalized mystery travel a well-known Nordic concept that is easy to use, exciting to experience and built around the customer. We want every NoClue trip to feel like more than just transportation and accommodation — it should be a story that starts before the traveller even knows where they are going.
