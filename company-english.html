NOCLUE ABROAD — COMPANY OVERVIEW

Travel without knowing where you're going.

NoClue Abroad is a Nordic surprise-travel service. Customers tell us what kind of trip they want, including dates, budget, weather and interests. We use this information to create a suitable trip while keeping the destination a surprise until the right moment.

01 — THE IDEA

You choose the feeling. We find the place.

The service is designed to remove the hardest part of travel planning. Instead of comparing dozens of destinations, hotels and activities, the customer gives us their wishes and we build a complete travel experience around them. Before departure, the customer receives the necessary information and a packing list, while the destination remains a surprise.

1. Tell us what you want

Choose your dates, budget, weather preferences and interests such as golf, swimming, food, cities or nature.

2. We plan the trip

We find destinations, flights, accommodation and activities that fit the wishes and budget.

3. Experience the surprise

The destination is revealed at the agreed moment, making the journey more exciting from the beginning.

02 — OUR CUSTOMERS & POSITIONING

A new kind of travel for Nordic customers.

Our main audience is busy people who want to travel but do not want to spend hours planning. Another important audience is young and adventurous travelers who want new experiences. Our concept focuses on the Nordic market, with departures from countries such as Finland and Sweden.

Our goal is to offer a youthful Nordic perspective on mystery travel and compete through a strong combination of personalization, surprise, activities and good price-quality.

03 — BUSINESS MODEL

Small team, digital service, scalable idea.

At the beginning, the company can operate with a small team. Employees research destinations and activities, build customer trips and make sure the overall experience works. Digital tools and AI can support research, comparison and planning, while people make the final decisions.

Revenue comes from selling the surprise-trip packages. The business can grow as awareness increases and the planning process becomes more efficient.

04 — FINANCIAL PLAN

Start carefully and grow step by step.

The financial plan is built around controlled initial costs and a clear focus on the service itself. The current plan includes €2,000 for marketing. The main costs come from travel arrangements, accommodation and activities for customers, together with marketing and the basic costs of running the company.

The aim is to build the customer base gradually, test the concept, improve the service through feedback and increase sales as the brand becomes better known.

05 — SWOT SUMMARY

Our strengths, challenges and opportunities.

Strengths

A Nordic focus, a youthful perspective, personalized trips and the excitement of a surprise destination.

Weaknesses

The company is new and has less experience and brand awareness than established travel businesses.

Opportunities

Surprise travel can offer a new type of experience in the Nordic market and can reach customers who want easier planning.

Threats

The Nordic customer base is smaller than many larger international markets, which can limit growth if the company remains focused only on the Nordics.

06 — RESPONSIBILITY

Travel should be exciting and responsible.

Safety is an important part of choosing destinations and activities. We also consider environmental effects when planning trips. Where practical, we can favor lower-impact options such as trains on shorter journeys and responsible hotels and activities. Travel information, tickets and packing lists can be delivered digitally to reduce unnecessary material use.

07 — FEEDBACK & DEVELOPMENT

Built around a simple idea that can grow.

Peer feedback has highlighted the idea as interesting and distinctive. The surprise element can make travel more exciting while saving planning time. The concept can work especially well for young and adventurous travelers, while still being useful for people who simply want an easier way to organize a trip.

We want to keep developing the service based on customer feedback, real travel data and changing customer needs.

08 — THE FUTURE

Less planning. More experience.

NoClue Abroad combines travel, technology and surprise into one simple service. Our long-term goal is to make personalized mystery travel a recognizable Nordic concept: safe, responsible, easy to buy and exciting to experience.
