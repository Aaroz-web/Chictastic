OUR COMPANY

Travel without knowing where you're going.

NoClue Abroad is a Nordic surprise-travel concept. Customers tell us their dates, budget and interests, and we create a trip around those wishes. The destination stays a surprise until the right moment.

01 — OUR IDEA

You choose the feeling. We find the place.

Our goal is to make travel planning easier while making the trip itself more exciting. Instead of spending hours comparing destinations, hotels and activities, the customer gives us the most important wishes and we build a suitable travel experience around them.

Your wishes

Dates, budget, weather and interests give us the starting point.

Our planning

We combine the wishes into a practical and memorable trip.

The surprise

The destination stays hidden, adding excitement to the journey.

02 — WHO WE ARE FOR

For people who want to travel differently.

NoClue Abroad is aimed at busy travelers who do not want to plan everything themselves, as well as young and adventurous people looking for something new. We want to bring a fresh Nordic perspective to surprise travel.

03 — HOW WE WORK

People, technology and good travel ideas.

We combine human planning with digital tools and AI. Technology can help us find and compare suitable options, while people make sure the final trip makes sense and fits the customer's wishes.

04 — OUR VALUES

Simple, exciting and responsible.

Safety, thoughtful choices and responsible travel are important parts of our concept. We aim to choose suitable destinations, activities and travel options while keeping the experience exciting and easy for the customer.
