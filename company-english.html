OUR COMPANY

Travel should be exciting, not complicated.

NoClue Abroad is a Nordic travel company that creates personalized surprise trips for people who want to travel without planning everything themselves.

01 — THE IDEA

A new way to travel.

Customers tell us their wishes, budget, travel dates and interests, and we use them to create a suitable trip.

02 — HOW IT WORKS

You know what you want.
We find where.

We look for destinations, flights, accommodation and activities that offer good value for money, while keeping the destination a surprise until the agreed reveal moment.

03 — OUR CUSTOMERS

Made for curious travellers.

Our service is designed for busy people who do not want to spend hours planning, as well as adventurous travellers who enjoy discovering something unexpected.

04 — OUR POSITION

A Nordic view on mystery travel.

We focus on customers from Finland and the Nordic countries and want to build a fresh, youthful and recognizable Nordic travel brand.

05 — RESPONSIBILITY

Travel with care.

Safety and responsible choices are important parts of how we plan our trips, including the destinations and experiences we recommend.

06 — THE FUTURE

More experience.
Less planning.

Our goal is to make personalized mystery travel a well-known Nordic concept that is easy to use, exciting to experience and built around the customer.
